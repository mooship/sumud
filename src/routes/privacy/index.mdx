_Last updated: 8 September 2026_

Sumud is a non-commercial project. It doesn't have an advertising business, doesn't sell data,
and doesn't run any third-party tracking or advertising scripts. Here's what actually happens
when you visit.

## Analytics

This site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see,
in aggregate, which pages get read. It's enabled at the network level rather than through a
script that runs in your browser, and it's built specifically not to track you: it doesn't use
cookies, doesn't set any persistent identifier, and doesn't fingerprint your device across
visits or sites. It can't tell one of your visits from another, and it can't follow you to a
different website. What it produces is aggregate numbers -- how many people read a given page,
roughly where from, on what kind of device -- not a profile of any individual visitor.

## Cookies and local storage

Sumud itself doesn't set any cookies. Your browser may keep a preference such as your system
dark/light mode locally, but that's your browser's own behaviour, not something this site reads
or stores.

## Hosting

The site is served by [Cloudflare Workers](https://workers.cloudflare.com/). As with any web
host, Cloudflare's network necessarily processes standard connection data (your IP address,
browser user agent, and similar) to deliver each page -- this is how the web works, not
something specific to or added by this site. See
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for how they handle
that at the infrastructure level.

## Links to other sites

Sumud links out to external organisations (UN agencies, human rights groups, humanitarian
charities) and to GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy practices, independent of this one
-- this policy only covers sumud.timothybrits.co.za itself.

## Corrections and issues

If you flag a correction via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mooship/sumud/issues), that
conversation happens on GitHub and is subject to
[GitHub's own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement),
not this one.

## Changes

If this policy changes in any material way, this page will be updated and the date above will
change with it.
