Sumud is an independent, non-commercial project. It exists because Palestine is one of the most written-about places on earth and, at the same time, one of the least well understood as a continuous story. Coverage tends to arrive in bursts, tied to whichever week's violence has made international news, with little of the history that explains how things got there and little space for the culture of the people living through it. This site tries to hold the longer view: Ottoman Palestine, the British Mandate, the Nakba, the decades of exile and occupation that followed, and the Palestine of today, alongside the language, food, craft and literature that have carried Palestinian identity through all of it.

## On sourcing and accuracy

This project draws on mainstream historical scholarship, UN and archival records, and reporting from human rights organisations across the political spectrum -- Israeli, Palestinian and international. The full list of the kinds of sources used is on the [sources page](/sources/). Where historians genuinely disagree about what happened, or why, this site tries to say so rather than present one account as beyond dispute. Where casualty or displacement figures are contested, they are attributed to their source.

This is a synthesis written by one person, not a peer-reviewed academic work, and it will contain mistakes. If you find one -- a wrong date, a misattributed quote, a figure that has been superseded by better information -- please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mooship/sumud/issues/new). Corrections are genuinely welcome, and this site will be updated when they're warranted.

## On perspective

This site does not pretend to be written from nowhere. It centres Palestinian history and the Palestinian experience of the past century, because that is the story most often left out, simplified, or told only from the outside. That is a choice about emphasis, not licence to get facts wrong -- the two 1948 chapters, for instance, describe both the Nakba and Israel's War of Independence as real and legitimate ways different people remember the same events, because they are.

## How this is built

Sumud is a static-first site built with [Qwik](https://qwik.dev/) and TypeScript, styled with [vanilla-extract](https://vanilla-extract.style/), and hosted on [Cloudflare Workers](https://workers.cloudflare.com/). It carries no third-party trackers or advertising. The source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/mooship/sumud).
